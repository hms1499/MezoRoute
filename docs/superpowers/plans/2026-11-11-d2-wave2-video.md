# D2 Wave 2 Demo Video Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Produce `video/out/mezoroute-wave2-demo.mp4`: a ≈ 2:08 (≤ 2:15) 1080p product video of the live app, 12 scenes with a chip per scene, spotlight focus, five real testnet transactions, dark theme, and the user's Envato track as music.

**Architecture:** The D1 tool, rebuilt (its code was deleted on 5 Nov; `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-d1-demo-video.md` Tasks 1–4 and 7 describe interfaces that worked and are reused here unless this plan says otherwise). A local Node tool in `video/` (git-ignored): Playwright drives Chromium with a mock EIP-1193 wallet whose signing runs in Node; a CDP screencast recorder saves timestamped frames plus an event log (scenes, waits to cut, spotlight boxes, tx hashes); a render script cuts waits, overlays chip and spotlight PNGs, concatenates, and adds music with `ffmpeg`.

**Tech Stack:** Node 24 (ESM `.mjs`, `node:test`), Playwright 1.56 with Chromium at `~/Library/Caches/ms-playwright/chromium-1248` (`executablePath`), viem 2.57.2, ffmpeg/ffprobe (`/opt/homebrew/bin`), `cast`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-d2-wave2-video-design.md` (its storyboard table is the script).

## Global Constraints

- Everything for the video lives in `video/` (git-ignored). Never add files or dependencies to `frontend/` or `contracts/` for it. Committed: the spec, this plan, `docs/STATUS.md`, and later the README's video link.
- Output: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 `yuv420p`, AAC; total ≤ 135.0 s, target ≈ 128 s.
- App laid out at a 1440 × 810 CSS-px viewport, device scale factor 4/3; `localStorage["mezoroute.theme"] = "dark"`.
- Chip and spotlight exactly as D1's Global Constraints (chip `#0A1416` ≈ 88%, 1 px `#2DD4BF` border, Inter 500 `#E8F1EF`, bold Inter 600 `#2DD4BF`, fade 0.3 s; spotlight ≈ 68% black, pad 12 px, radius 16 px CSS, 0.5 s in/out, page never moves). At least one spotlight per scene.
- No title cards, zoom, voiceover, or wallet popups.
- Mezo testnet only for sends (chain `31611`, `0x7b7b`, RPC `https://rpc.test.mezo.org`); production `https://mezoroute.vercel.app`; wallet `0xd74f60bb70d2A8B412fF12d8ef0ffdaAe81B2B49`, key from `contracts/.env` read only inside Node; never print, log, write, or pass the key to the page.
- A take fails on: a staged number, a reverted tx, an error toast, an error state on screen, an uncaught page error, a skeleton/spinner held on screen, unreadable text, over 2:15. Third-party `console.error` lines are logged, not fatal.
- App fixes: stop, report, fix only after the user approves in chat (branch, test first, merge, push `main`), re-check production.
- Trove floor: the Trove's CR must stay ≥ 160% after Borrow & Deploy and Leverage; no chain manipulation beyond the one Trove top-up (`addColl`).
- Music: the first audio file in `video/music/`; extended without an audible seam when shorter than the video, fade in ≤ 1 s, fade out over the last 3 s, ≈ −19 LUFS. With no file, the render muxes a silent AAC track so the output format stays the same.

## Review Focus

- **Leverage blocked by the oracle bound at take time** (testnet pool several percent off the oracle): scene 9 detects the app's blocked/zero state and records the page unsent with the app's own message (Task 5 scene rule), instead of throwing.
- **Pending MEZO too small to read** right after Borrow & Deploy's stake: scene 6 waits until the pending MEZO shown is ≥ 0.01 or uses MEZO already pending (Task 5); preflight reports pending MEZO.
- **BTC price moves between takes so Borrow & Deploy or Leverage would cross 160%:** preflight computes the CR after a 100 MUSD borrow and refuses below 175% (Task 3 test).
- **Testnet RPC misses a mined tx:** every confirmation wait is a `waitCut` with a 120 s budget; slow confirmation is cut, not failed (D1 Task 4 test).
- **A quote expires (60 s) before Send:** scenes assert the primary button is enabled and re-review on an expired quote before sending (Task 5 scene helper).
